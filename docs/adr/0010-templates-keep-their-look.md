# A screen template keeps the look it was saved with

Decided 2026-11-11 (#428). A template is built from presets for its words, background and effects and may tweak them,
but it stores the look as saved: editing a preset later does not change the screens using it. An untouched template
whose preset changed shows "Update" on its card instead. Screens changing on their own during a service was worse
than having to update a template by hand.
