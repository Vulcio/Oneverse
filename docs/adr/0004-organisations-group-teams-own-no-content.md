# Organisations group teams; they never own songs or sets

An organisation (#241) is made of teams, and its "groups" are ordinary teams (a team may be part of one organisation
and sit under another team), not a model of their own, so everything a team does works inside an organisation
unchanged. Songs, songbooks, sets and arrangements stay owned by people and teams, never by an organisation
(confirmed 2026-10-27, #331); an organisation governs its teams' sets rather than owning them (2026-10-23). A team
that only plans (Kids service, Welcome) owns no songs. A server may have no organisations at all, and Songverse then
works exactly as on its own.

## Considered Options

- A separate Group model for organisations: rejected, it would duplicate membership, invites, calendars and roles.
- Organisation-owned songs: rejected, a song's rights and edits belong to the people and teams who play it.
