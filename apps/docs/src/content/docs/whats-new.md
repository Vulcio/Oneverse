What changed in each version, newest first. When your server is updated, its admins can tell you about it under the bell (see [Announcements](/admin/#announcements)).

## Next version (0.10)

### Announcements

- News from your server's admins - a new version, new features - and from your organisations' and teams' admins now arrives with your notifications: under the bell and on your devices. Choose how they reach you in your account's [notification settings](/account/#notifications).

### Sets and planning

- The set page is a running order: a table on a computer, one line per song on a phone, with each song's settings in a sheet.
- Transitions are easier to find: named columns, and **After this song** between songs and in Practice.
- Who can play shows on the set's page, with the parts people would like to play, the host and the music leader.
- Event types for a team's calendar and an organisation's groups, events without a set, required parts and open places anyone can take.
- Groups that only plan can have sets too.
- Each date has its own page, the same in Songverse, Plan and Oneverse, where its notifications lead (see [A date's page](/teams/#a-dates-page)); a date can be moved to another day with its answers, line-up and set.
- Oneverse has a home: sign in there and see your apps, your next dates to answer, open places, news, your teams and your organisation's links (see [Oneverse's home](/getting-started/#oneverses-home)).
- Planiverse is now [Oneverse Plan](/plan/), at plan.oneverse.top: same app, same pages, under the Oneverse name. Old planiverse.top links lead there.

### Songs and playing

- Pinch to zoom charts in Practice and Live, words and chords together.
- Song views without a box around them; the sections bar folds repeats (Intro ×4) and counts them.
- Autoscroll lets go while you scroll by hand, then eases back in.
- Recording on a phone: a slim bar while recording, Follow on or off, nothing else playing.
- What plays first loads first, and audio stays ready when you switch songs.
- Looks for the chart in Live: Plain, Blocks, Rail, High contrast, chosen by each player.
- Artists with several names are one artist: every name leads there.
- A built-in list of themes, seasons, occasions and genres to tag songs with.

### Screens and the Bible

- Set up a first screen in minutes; templates with calmer built-in effects, bigger words and settings that hide when they have no effect.
- Screens keep going when the network drops, and their keys can move the slides for everyone.
- Borrow a screen for a set from the operator's console; the running order can sit on the left, along the top or out of the way.
- Bible passages: books named as each version names them, verses picked from a grid, search through YouVersion.

### For admins

- Send email through any SMTP server, beside Resend.
- Report errors to Sentry or GlitchTip.
- Step-by-step guides in Admin > Metadata.
