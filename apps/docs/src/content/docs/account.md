Everything about you is under **Settings**: choose your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Settings** (in Oneverse and Oneverse Plan, it's under your picture). It's the same page in every app. On a computer its sections are listed down the side; on a phone, pick one from the list at the top.

- **Every app**: **Account** and **Notifications** - the same wherever you change them.
- **App settings**: **Songverse** and **Oneverse Plan**, each app's own. The app you're in shows its own; another app's opens in that app.

Links to the old pages (the Dashboard's settings, **Account settings**) bring you here.

## Profile and picture

- **First name** and **Last name** - how you appear to others: in teams, in sets you share, on songs you submit. The card shows the name others see.
- **Upload a picture** - any common image format. You crop it to a square; it's saved at up to 512×512.

## Language and appearance

Its own card, under your profile, in every app:

- **Language** - every app, email and notification uses it.
- **Appearance** - **Light**, **Dark** or **Auto**. Auto follows each device's own choice. Until you pick one, each device keeps what it had. It's kept on your account, so every app and device follows it; Live in Songverse stays dark. The buttons in Oneverse's top corner and the moon in Songverse's sidebar change it too.

On the sign-in page, the eye in a password field shows what you typed.

## Sign-in

Your email, password and passkeys belong to your **Oneverse** account, the one you sign in to every app with. They're changed at Oneverse, under **Settings** > **Account**; elsewhere, the **Sign-in** card's **Change at Oneverse** opens it.

### Email

To change it, enter the **New email** and choose **Change email**. We first ask your current address to approve the change, then send a confirmation link to the new one. Your email changes once you open it.

### Password

**Change password** asks your **Current password** and a **New password**; your other devices are signed out. When you sign in without one (a passkey or Google), the card says so.

### Passkeys

A passkey lets you sign in without a password, with your device's fingerprint, face or screen lock.

1. Under **Passkeys**, give it a name if you like ("My laptop").
2. Choose **Add a passkey** and follow your device's prompt.

Add one on each device you use. You can remove a passkey at any time.

## Summaries

**Account** also sums up the rest: how many notification sections reach you, and your quiet hours (**Open** goes to **Notifications**), and your coming days away (**Change** goes to the **Oneverse Plan** settings).

## Notifications

Everything you're told shows under the bell at the top of every page (see [Notifications](/teams/#notifications)): the bell always shows everything. **Settings** > **Notifications** chooses what also reaches you by **Push** (your devices) and by **Email**, in sections:

- **Dates you're on** - a date cancelled, changed or moved, the day before, its set ready.
- **Line-ups and answers** - you're put on a part or taken off, asked for your answers, someone needs a replacement or takes your place.
- **Teams you run** (only if you're an admin of a team or organisation) - who declined, offered, joined or asked, and bookings.
- **Songs and sets** - songs and songbooks shared with you, replies to your notes, your suggestions and catalogue submissions reviewed, a part recorded in your multitrack.
- **People and teams** - a request to connect, your role in a team.
- **Organisations** - the organisation you asked for, made or declined.
- **News** - from this server's admins (a new version, see [What's new](/whats-new/)), your organisations and your teams: pushed, but not emailed until you turn **Email** on for it.

Each section's **Push** and **Email** switch turns all its kinds on or off at once; it reads **2 of 5** when only some are on. **Choose one by one** opens the section to tick each kind. **Everything**, at the top, turns every section on or off at once. Several at once come as one email, or one notification. When the server doesn't send them by email or to devices, the page says so.

**Turn on notifications on this device** asks your browser for permission, then lists the device under **Your devices** - do it on each phone or computer you want them on. The bell offers it too: on a device where they aren't on yet, its list starts with **Get them on this device too** - **Turn on**, or **Not now** to stop asking on this device (or, when the browser blocks them, it says so). **Notification settings**, at the bottom of the bell's list, opens this section from anywhere. On an iPhone or iPad, add Songverse to the home screen first (Share, then Add to Home Screen) and turn them on from there. **Send a test notification** checks they arrive; **Turn off on this device**, or the bin beside a device, stops them there. A device whose browser stopped them is removed by itself; one the last push didn't reach says why, in red under it. If a test doesn't show, close every Songverse tab (or quit the app) and open it again: a new version of Songverse takes over a browser only once all its tabs are closed.

**Quiet hours** keep your devices silent at night (22:00 to 07:00 to begin with, in your time zone - change **From** and **To**): what comes in then shows when they end. The bell and emails aren't held back.

## Songverse

**Settings** > **Songverse** has what is Songverse's own: what you play or do, how charts read, your storage and your backups.

### What you play or do

Tell your teams what you play or do - **Instruments** (vocals, guitars, keys, drums, harp, accordion, cajón… and any your admin added) and **Other roles** (technician, media operator, sound engineer). They're shown next to your name in team member lists.

Until you've said, Songverse asks **What do you play or do?** after you sign up, with the instruments and roles to tick and a photo to add. **Skip for now** asks again in a month, or when you join a team. Your dashboard shows **Complete your profile** until you have.

### Chart display

How chords read on every chart you open:

- **Chord names** - letters (C D E), solfège (Do Ré Mi) Nashville numbers (1 4 5) or Roman numerals (I IV V): see [Nashville numbers and chord colours](/versions/#nashville-numbers-and-chord-colours).
- **Chord colours** - **Plain**, or **By chord type**.
- **With a capo** - chords as they sound, or as the shapes to play. Shapes are written in *italics*, so they're never taken for the chords you hear, and a song with a capo shows it as a red pill - **⚠ Capo 1 · chords as shapes** - in Practice and Live.
- **Chord diagrams** - **None**, **Guitar**, **Ukulele** or **Piano**: how to play the song's chords, beside its chart (see [Chord diagrams](/versions/#chord-diagrams)).
- **Songs read as** - **Chords and lyrics**, **Chord grid (bars)** (see [Chord grid](/metronome/#chord-grid)), **Words, for singers** (see [Words, for singers](/library/#words-for-singers)), **Drums, for drummers** (see [Drums, for drummers](/library/#drums-for-drummers)), or **PDF, when the song has one**: how songs show in Practice and Live unless you choose otherwise for a song (see [Playing a set live](/sets/#playing-a-set-live)). A song without a PDF shows its chart instead.

They're where every mode starts: the [Display](/display/) panel changes them for one mode - Edit, Practice or Live - and its **↺** puts that mode back to these. Hiding chords and simpler chords are set per song, from its chart - see [Your own view of a chart](/versions/#your-own-view-of-a-chart).

### Storage

Files you upload to your own songs (PDFs, audio, ChordPro files) count toward your storage limit; what you add to a team's songs counts toward the team's pool instead. The **Storage** card shows how much you use. Ask an admin if you need more: they give storage tiers as roles.

### Backups

The **Backups** card makes an archive of everything you own, now or every week, and imports one back: see [Backups](/backups/).

## Oneverse Plan

**Settings** > **Oneverse Plan**, in Oneverse Plan and in Songverse:

- **Availability** - your days away, for all your teams: every date in them reads **Can't make it** unless you answer it (see [Availability](/teams/#availability)). **My calendar** keeps its own **Availability** too.
- **First day of the week** - where every calendar's weeks start: **Automatic (from the language)** - Sunday in English, Monday in French - or always **Monday**, **Sunday** or **Saturday**. Songverse's calendar follows it too.
- **Calendar link** - your dates in another calendar app (see [Your calendar link](/teams/#your-calendar-link)).
