title: Bible
description: Read a Bible passage in your team's sidebar, in any version and language, and add it to a set.
---

**Bible**, in the sidebar's **Tools**, opens a Bible to read a passage and add it to a set. It's there once a team you're in turns it on, or the organisation that team is part of (see [Turning it on](#turning-it-on)).

## Picking a passage

- **Language**: the versions' language, yours to begin with. Type to search, and pick any other.
- **Version**: the Bibles imported on this server (**On this server**) and, when the server's admin has set it up, YouVersion's versions in that language. Type its name or abbreviation to find it.
- **Book**: type to search, or pick it from the list. **Bible order** lists the books as the Bible does, the **Old Testament** then the **New Testament**; **A-Z** lists them by name. Your choice is kept on this device.
- **Chapter**: picking a book opens its chapters, to pick one.
- **Verses**: the first and the last, or none for the whole chapter.

The book names and everything else stay in your language, whatever the version's language. What you pick is written in **Passage** ("John 3:16-18"). You can also type it there, as people write it ("Ps 23:1-4", "Jean 3,16"), then **Read**.

The passage shows with its version's copyright line. A YouVersion passage also has a link to read it on YouVersion.

## Adding it to a set

A passage from a Bible imported on the server can go into a set. Under the passage, **Add to a set** lists the sets you can change that are still to come: pick one, then its **Screen template**, and **Add to the set**. The set keeps the reference, never a copy of the text, and shows it like a passage added from the set's page (see [Sets](/sets/)). A YouVersion passage can't go into a set yet, until each version's licence says where its text may go.

**Screen template** is the template the [screens](/screens/) show the passage with while it's presented; the rest of the set keeps the screen's own. Those **For scripture** come first - the built-in **Scripture** and the templates tagged **Scripture** - then **Other templates**, each with how many verses it puts on a slide. **The screen's own template** shows it like the rest of the set, a verse a slide. The last one you picked is kept on this device.

## Turning it on

The Bible is off until an admin turns it on:

- On a team's page, its admins tick **Show the Bible in the sidebar**, under **Bible**, for the team's members.
- On an organisation's page, its admins do the same for the members of all its teams. A team in that organisation shows it as already on.

The Bibles themselves come from the server's admin, in **Admin > Bibles** (see [Admin](/admin/#bibles)).
