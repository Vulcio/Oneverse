---
title: Bible
description: Read a Bible passage in your team's sidebar, in any version and language, and add it to a set.
---

**Bible**, in the sidebar's **Tools**, opens a Bible to read a passage and add it to a set. It's there once a team you're in turns it on, or the organisation that team is part of (see [Turning it on](#turning-it-on)).

## Picking a passage

- **Language**: the versions' language, yours to begin with. Type to search, and pick any other.
- **Version**: the Bibles imported on this server (**On this server**) and, when the server's admin has set it up, YouVersion's versions in that language. Type its name or abbreviation to find it.
- **Book**: type to search, or pick it from the list. **Bible order** lists the books as the Bible does, the **Old Testament** then the **New Testament**; **A-Z** lists them by name. Your choice is kept on this device.
- **Chapter**: picking a book opens its chapters, to pick one.
- **Verses**: picking a chapter opens its verses, as many as the version has: tap the first, then the last (the same one again for a single verse). **Whole chapter** reads all of it. A few chapters are numbered differently from one Bible to another (a psalm's title counted as its first verse, for example): the grid shows the version's own numbering.

The books are named as the version names them, in its language: "Juan" in a Spanish version. Until the version's own list has come, and for a version that has none, they're named in your language. What you pick is written in **Passage** ("Juan 3:16-18"). You can also type it there, as people write it, in English, in French or with the version's own names ("Ps 23:1-4", "Jean 3,16", "Salmos 23"), then **Read**.

## Searching

Below the picker, **Search** finds verses in a YouVersion version: type words ("love your enemies"), a theme ("peace") or a reference, then **Search**.

- **Verses**: the verses found, as references. Tap one to read it below and add it to a set. **More verses** shows more of them.
- **Related topics**: themes close to what you searched, with narrower ones beside them. Tap one to search for it.
- **Did you mean**: other spellings, to search instead. When YouVersion corrected a likely typo, the results say what they're for ("Showing results for love.").
- What YouVersion took your search for is shown beside them: words in verses, a theme, or a reference. A reference has its own **Read** button.

Search works in YouVersion's versions only, not in the Bibles imported on the server. YouVersion allows a limited number of requests an hour, so it searches when you press **Search**, never as you type, and a verse's text is only asked for when you tap it.

The passage shows with its version's copyright line. A YouVersion passage also has a link to read it on YouVersion.

## Adding it to a set

A passage can go into a set, from a Bible imported on the server or from YouVersion. Under the passage, **Add to a set** lists the sets you can change that are still to come: pick one, then its **Screen template**, and **Add to the set**. The set keeps the reference, never a copy of the text, and shows it like a passage added from the set's page (see [Sets](/sets/)). A YouVersion passage is read from YouVersion, on the screens too, and kept only as long as YouVersion allows (see [Admin](/admin/#bibles)).

**Screen template** is the template the [screens](/screens/) show the passage with while it's presented; the rest of the set keeps the screen's own. Those **For scripture** come first - the built-in **Scripture** and the templates tagged **Scripture** - then **Other templates**, each with how many verses it puts on a slide; how a template cuts a passage into slides and shows its verse numbers is set in its editor (see [Templates](/screens/#templates)). **The screen's own template** shows it with the screen's **Template for Bible passages** (or its template for songs), a verse a slide. The last one you picked is kept on this device.

## Turning it on

The Bible is off until an admin turns it on:

- On a team's page, its admins tick **Show the Bible in the sidebar**, under **Bible**, for the team's members.
- On an organisation's page, its admins do the same for the members of all its teams. A team in that organisation shows it as already on.

The Bibles themselves come from the server's admin, in **Admin > Bibles** (see [Admin](/admin/#bibles)).
