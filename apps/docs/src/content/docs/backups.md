title: Backups
description: Keep a copy of everything you, your team or your organisation own - an archive made now or every week - and import one back.
---

Songverse keeps your songs safe on its server, and its admins back the server up. Backups here are yours: an archive of everything you own, to download and keep where you like, and to bring back - on this server or another.

## What's in an archive

**Backups** is in your dashboard's settings. **Make an archive now** puts together, in one zip file:

- your own songs, with their charts, details, credits, tags, links and files (attachments, recordings, stems, artwork);
- your arrangements, your songbooks and your sets (their text and passage items too: a passage is its reference, never the Bible's text);
- the notes you wrote for yourself, and the notes on your songbooks and sets;
- each song as ChordPro as well, to read anywhere.

Songs only shared with you aren't in it (they're someone else's), nor someone else's private files on your songs. Making it can take a moment: the card says when it's ready, and **Download** gets it. An archive can be downloaded for two weeks.

Tick **Make one every week** and a new one is made each week; the last two weekly ones are kept.

## A team's, an organisation's

A team's admins find **Backups** on the team's page: the archive holds what the team owns - its songs, arrangements, songbooks, sets and team notes. An organisation's admins find it on the organisation's page: everything its teams own, team by team.

## Importing one back

**Import an archive** and choose the zip file. Everything in it comes in as new, owned by you (or the team, or the organisation's teams), and **nothing already here is changed**:

- what's already here, the same, is used as it is - so importing the same archive twice adds nothing;
- a songbook whose name you already use comes in as "… (imported)";
- in an organisation, each team's content goes to its team there (the same team, or one with the same name), or to a new team made for it, with you as its admin.

When it's done, the card says what came in, what was already there, and what couldn't come in and why - a song in a set that isn't on this server, say, or one you can't see. Notes others wrote come in as yours, starting with their name.
